## General Guidance 

{% capture text %}
**NOTE:** Before scanning materials for an inquiry, check the **Archive drive** to see whether something has already been scanned! If you don't have access to the drive, contact the Digital Archivist.
{% endcapture %}

{% include alert.html text=text color="warning" %}

When a patron contacts Spec and their inquiry is detailed, answering their questions or requests may be straightforward.

However, sometimes a patron request isn't very clear. In those cases, asking for clarification or even asking more questions about the subject might help narrow down a scope of research.

Then there are times when a patron has a very broad topic and doesn't quite know what they're looking for. In this situation it might be beneficial to send the patron some How-To PDFs. That way patrons can do their own search, and then contact us with the materials they'd like to view.

1. [How-To Search Archives West](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/Storage-Library/Documents/spec/Marketing%20and%20Communications/How-To%20Search%20AW.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=wXhZRF)
2. [How-To Search the Library Catalog](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/Storage-Library/Documents/spec/Marketing%20and%20Communications/How-To%20Search%20Library%20Catalog%20(1).pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=cdEo9O)
3. [How-To Search Digital Collections](https://vandalsuidaho.sharepoint.com/:b:/r/sites/Storage-Library/Documents/spec/Policies%20and%20Procedures/How-To%20for%20Patrons/How-To%20Search%20Digital%20Collections.pdf?csf=1&web=1&e=gJNPL3)

When prompted to find a **digital assets**, see whether the finding aid record includes the file names. Look for these in the Archive Drive. Ask colleagues for help if issues arise.

## Directing a Patron to Use Archives West

Example language: 

*Make sure links work*

{:.table .table-bordered}
| We would be glad to help you access any materials in our archives that pertain to your research. To begin, you can review the finding aids for our collections by visiting Archives West, the consortium of regional archives we belong to. If in your search you find any materials related to [*research topic*], please send us an email to make an appointment to see the materials. We would need to know the collection number and the folders or boxes you want to see. <br><br>It might be worth expanding your search in Archives West to other repositories in the region. You can do so by changing the filter at the top of the search box. <br><br>We recently produced a tutorial to introduce the basics of using Archives West. It can be found here: [https://youtu.be/P_aH4S3MZrg?si=qrvhYn3daUkdr0lt](https://youtu.be/P_aH4S3MZrg?si=qrvhYn3daUkdr0lt). <br><br>Please reach out if we can be of further assistance.|


## Boilerplate Language for Complex Requests

<div class="border p-3" markdown="1">
 Dear ____,<br><br>I am glad that your initial research has indicated that the University of Idaho's Library archives may contain material relevant to your project. The historic records preserved by our department, Special Collections and Archives, are rich and diverse. Those of us working in the archive are ready to help you access materials that can further your research, whether you visit in person or request digital copies from afar.
 
 You can explore the records that are available for viewing in a few ways. For manuscript and university collections (i.e. a group of unique, mostly unpublished primary source materials created or accumulated by a specific person or group), search our finding aids on [Archives West](https://archiveswest.orbiscascade.org/search.php?r=idu). We created a short tutorial to help get folks started using that platform - [Searching Archival Records at the University of Idaho Library](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_aH4S3MZrg). To search the rare books held in our department, use the Library's [general catalog search bar](https://alliance-uidaho.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/search?tab=Everything&search_scope=DN_and_CI&vid=01ALLIANCE_UID:UID&query=any,contains,). All of our digitized material, including historic photographs, university yearbooks and newspapers, and oral histories, is available on our [Digital Collections site](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/digital/).
 
 If you find listings for items that you would like to see, in person or via scans, please send an email to libspec@uidaho.edu. We will need to know when you would like to make an appointment or if you are requesting digital copies. Please note that we can generally provide assistance and copies free of charge, within reason. Inquiries that require 30 minutes or less to complete and reproduction requests that total less than 25 pages can generally be provided at no charge. When a request exceeds the parameters listed above, we may assess a reproduction fee of $25 for each additional half-hour increment. Due to time and capacity constraints, we cannot guarantee that we will be able to fill all requests. To better serve you, we ask that you provide the following information about any items that you would like to inspect.<br><br>For materials found in Archives West:

- Title and Collection Number 
- Box and Folder Numbers
- Names of specific items (if available)

For materials found in the Library's book catalog:

- Book Title
- Call Number

Please note that the primary function of our department is to connect users with records that could further their research. We are unable to provide substantial assistance completing research tasks, which includes exhaustive searches of archival collections. While items relevant to your project may exist in collections held in the archive, it is the work of researchers to explore those records in their totality.

You can learn more about how our department can assist you in your research by visiting our website, including ["Plan Your Visit,"](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/special-collections/plan.html) ["Reproduction Requests,"](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/special-collections/policies.html#reproduction-requests) and ["Frequently Asked Questions."](https://www.lib.uidaho.edu/special-collections/faq.html)

Wishing you success in your research venture,
